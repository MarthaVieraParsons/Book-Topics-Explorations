# Chapter 16: Your Buyer Checklist & FAQs

You've made it through the full buying process — now let's pull it all together into something you can actually use, step by step.

## Your Pull-Out Buyer Checklist

**Before You Start Looking**
- [ ] Reviewed credit reports and addressed any errors
- [ ] Built a realistic personal budget, separate from my maximum qualifying amount
- [ ] Started the pre-approval process with a trusted lender
- [ ] Identified my must-haves vs. nice-to-haves
- [ ] Narrowed down which communities fit my lifestyle and budget

**While Touring and Offering**
- [ ] Attended showings with a clear sense of my true ceiling price
- [ ] Discussed offer strategy (price, terms, contingencies) before submitting
- [ ] Understood exactly what my earnest money deposit covers and how it's protected

**Under Contract**
- [ ] Scheduled general and any needed specialty inspections
- [ ] Reviewed HOA documents and financials, if applicable
- [ ] Confirmed my lender has everything needed to avoid underwriting delays
- [ ] Reviewed the appraisal once completed

**Before Closing**
- [ ] Reviewed my Closing Disclosure at least three days before closing
- [ ] Confirmed wiring instructions by phone through a verified number — never by email alone
- [ ] Completed my final walkthrough
- [ ] Confirmed homeowners insurance is active as of closing day

**After Closing**
- [ ] Transferred or activated all utilities
- [ ] Updated my address everywhere it matters
- [ ] Located main shut-offs and tested HVAC thoroughly
- [ ] Saved digital and physical copies of all closing documents

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much do I actually need for a down payment?**
It depends entirely on your loan type — from 0% for eligible VA loans, to 3-3.5% for conventional and FHA loans, up to 20%+ to avoid mortgage insurance on a conventional loan. We'll walk through your specific options in Chapter 4.

**How long does the whole process typically take?**
From starting your search to closing, plan on roughly two to four months for most buyers, though this varies widely based on how quickly you find the right home and your specific financing path.

**What if I'm relocating and need to time my move with my current home's sale?**
This is extremely common, and there are several ways to structure it depending on your risk tolerance and financial flexibility — we covered this directly in Chapters 2 and 12, and I'm always happy to talk through your specific situation.

**Do I really need a home inspection if the home looks great?**
Yes. Some of the most significant issues — electrical, HVAC capacity, roof age, foundation concerns — aren't visible to an untrained eye, no matter how well-maintained a home appears.

**What happens if I need to back out after my offer is accepted?**
This depends on your contract's specific contingencies and where you are in the timeline. Acting within your contingency periods generally protects your earnest money; acting outside them may not. I'll always make sure you know exactly where you stand.

**Can I really buy a home here without visiting in person?**
Yes — Chapter 13 walks through exactly how long-distance buyers do this successfully, with the right tools and the right team on the ground.
